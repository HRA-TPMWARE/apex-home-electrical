Electrical advice without the jargon.

Practical guidance for common domestic electrical problems.

RCD keeps tripping?

Repeated tripping can indicate a fault on a circuit or appliance. Avoid repeatedly resetting it without investigating the cause.

Hot or damaged accessories

If a socket, switch or fitting is unusually hot, damaged or smells burnt, stop using it and get it checked.

Lighting faults

Flickering, intermittent or repeatedly failing lighting can have several causes.

Important: never remove covers or expose live electrical parts to take a photo or investigate a fault.
