## Strategy
Service pages target specific job intent. Location pages target broad local electrician intent and link into the service pages.
Avoid generating every possible service × town combination unless Search Console later shows real demand.
After launch, use Google Search Console impressions, queries, clicks and average position to decide which new landing pages or advice articles deserve priority.
